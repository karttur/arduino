### MantiSpectra

Filter based spectral sensor with 16 bands covering 850-1700 nm. For spectral details see [Ou et la., 2022, Handheld NIR Spectral Sensor Module Based on a Fully-Integrated Detector Array](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/22/18/7027).

### Scanspectrum

The [ScanSpectrum](https://scanspectrum.qed.ai) is a handheld and mobile phone operated grating spectrometer that covers the wavelength range 400-1000 nm. It can be used for both reflectance and transmittance spectroscopy. An android operated smartphone is connected using a usb-cable and used for edge computing and graphical user interface.

### Frauenhofer MEMS

950-1900 nm with a spectral resolution of 10 nm. https://www.ipms.fraunhofer.de/en/Components-and-Systems/Components-and-Systems-Sensors/Optical-Sensors/MEMS-based-spectroscopy.html, [datasheet](https://www.ipms.fraunhofer.de/content/dam/ipms/en/products/ENV/Scanning%20Mirror%20Micro%20Spectrometer.pdf).
